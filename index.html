Vathography Studio

Draw with distance.

Vathography turns the hidden depth of a photograph into the artwork itself. An AI model estimates how far away every point is; then you choose which distances light up.

Everything runs on your device. Photos never leave it. JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF.
